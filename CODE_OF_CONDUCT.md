# Code of conduct

Proofstone is a technical project for people who write Minecraft plugins, mods and add-ons. We want it to stay a place where
people of every background and experience level can ask, answer and contribute.

## Our standard

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree about code and decisions, not about people.
- Give feedback that is specific and kind. Accept feedback the same way.
- Keep to the topic of the project in issues and PRs.
- Welcome newcomers. Agents acting for humans are held to the same standard as humans.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discrimination, sexualised language or attention, doxxing, threats, spam, or publishing
a person's private information.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to the repository, its issues, PRs, discussions and any space where someone represents the project.
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, and block contributors who break this code.

Report a problem through **GitHub private vulnerability reporting** (<https://github.com/H7KZ/Proofstone/security/advisories/new>,
write "conduct" in the title). Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's identity is not shared without consent.
